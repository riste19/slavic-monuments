A RESTORATION / THE STORY BEHIND THE STONE

Preserve the meaning.
Renew the memorial.

A monument more than 35 years old.
A headstone worth keeping.

The original headstone.
A stronger foundation.

In his project notes, Nenad describes a single monument that had begun to deteriorate after more than 35 years, with inadequate footings contributing to the problem.

  1. Carefully dismantledThe monument was taken apart and the original headstone saved.
  2. Polished and re-inscribedThe existing headstone was retained and its lettering renewed.
  3. Rebuilt from the foundationA new engineered footing, kerbing and niche completed the restoration.

07 / A LITTLE CLARITY

Before
we begin.

Ask your own question ↗
01Where do I start?+

Begin with the cemetery name and the type of memorial you have in mind. If you are unsure, select “Help me choose” in the enquiry and share what you know.

02Can I bring my own ideas?+

Yes. A reference photograph, an inscription idea or a description can help begin the conversation. Ask Nenad about what can be made and what the cemetery permits.

03Are these photographs of your work?+

Yes. All project photographs on this preview show Slavic Monuments’ own work, sourced from Nenad’s portfolio. The photographs show completed projects; each new memorial is designed and quoted individually.

04How do I get a quote?+

Use the enquiry builder to save a preview request for Riki, or contact Nenad directly for actual services. Pricing and timeframes need his confirmation.

05Can I arrange this from elsewhere?+

You can begin by phone or email from wherever you are. Include the cemetery location and ask Nenad to confirm coverage and the arrangements needed.
